My Story

Brenda Burk is a registered nurse with a Master of Science in Nursing (MSN) and a Healing Touch Certified Practitioner (HTCP). With decades of experience in both conventional healthcare and holistic healing arts, Brenda brings a rare and beautiful blend of clinical knowledge and spiritual wisdom to her practice.

Her journey into energy healing began during her nursing career, where she witnessed firsthand the limits of conventional medicine in addressing the whole person — body, mind, and spirit. Drawn to explore deeper dimensions of healing, Brenda pursued extensive training in Healing Touch, Sound Healing, Crystal Healing, Labyrinth facilitation, and Sacred Geometry.

Today, Brenda serves clients from across the Charlotte, NC region and beyond, offering a sanctuary of stillness, compassion, and transformative healing. Her sessions are deeply personalized, honoring each client's unique journey and intention.
