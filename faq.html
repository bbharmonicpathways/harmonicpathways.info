Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our healing services

We understand you may have questions about energy healing and what to expect. Below are answers to the most common questions we receive. If you don't find what you're looking for, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.

✨ General Questions

Energy healing is a holistic practice that works with the body's natural energy systems to promote balance, relaxation, and well-being. Practitioners use various techniques to support the flow of energy throughout the body, helping to reduce stress, ease discomfort, and encourage the body's natural healing processes. Energy healing is considered a complementary practice and works alongside conventional medical care.

Yes, energy healing is gentle and non-invasive. It is safe for people of all ages, including children and the elderly. Sessions are deeply relaxing and carry no known risks. Energy healing is not a substitute for medical treatment, but it can be a wonderful complement to conventional care. Always consult your healthcare provider for medical concerns.

No prior belief is required. Many clients come with an open but skeptical mind and still experience profound relaxation and benefit. We simply ask that you come with an open heart and a willingness to receive. The healing process is unique to each individual, and your experience will be your own.

Every person's journey is unique. Some clients feel significant shifts after just one session, while others benefit from a series of sessions over time. We recommend starting with an initial session and then discussing a personalized plan based on your goals and how you respond. Many clients choose to continue sessions as part of their ongoing wellness routine.

🤲 Healing Touch

Healing Touch is a gentle, heart-centered energy therapy that uses light touch or near-body techniques to support the body's natural healing process. During a session, you will lie fully clothed on a comfortable table while the practitioner works with your energy field. Sessions typically last 45-60 minutes and most clients feel deeply relaxed, peaceful, and refreshed afterward.

While both Healing Touch and Reiki are energy healing modalities, they are distinct practices. Healing Touch is a nurse-developed, evidence-based program with a structured curriculum and certification process. It uses a variety of specific techniques and has been widely studied in clinical settings. Reiki is a Japanese technique with its own lineage and methods. Both are valuable and complementary practices.

Please wear comfortable, loose-fitting clothing. You will remain fully clothed throughout the session. We recommend avoiding heavy perfumes or fragrances out of consideration for sensitivities. Remove any heavy jewelry or accessories if you prefer, though this is not required.

🎵 Sound Healing

Sound healing sessions at BB's Harmonic Pathways may include Tibetan singing bowls, crystal singing bowls, tuning forks, chimes, and other resonant instruments. Each instrument produces unique frequencies and vibrations that interact with the body's energy field to promote deep relaxation and balance.

Many clients find sound healing to be profoundly effective for reducing anxiety and stress. The vibrations and frequencies produced during a session can help calm the nervous system, slow brainwave activity, and induce a deep meditative state. Regular sessions may support long-term stress management and emotional well-being.

💎 Crystal Healing

Crystals are believed to hold unique vibrational frequencies that can interact with the body's energy field. Different crystals are associated with different properties and intentions. During a crystal healing session, stones are carefully placed on or around the body to support energetic balance, release blockages, and promote a sense of harmony and well-being.

Absolutely not! No prior knowledge of crystals is needed. Your practitioner will guide you through the entire process, explain which crystals are being used and why, and answer any questions you may have. All you need to do is relax and receive.

🌀 Walking the Labyrinth

A labyrinth is a single, winding path that leads to a center and back out again. Unlike a maze, there are no dead ends or wrong turns — you simply follow the path. The labyrinth is an ancient symbol found across many cultures and traditions. Walking it is a moving meditation that invites reflection, clarity, and inner peace.

There is no right or wrong way to walk the labyrinth. Some people come with a specific intention or question in mind. Others simply walk and allow whatever arises to surface naturally. You may choose to walk slowly and mindfully, pause at the center for reflection, or simply enjoy the meditative rhythm of the path. Trust your own inner guidance.
